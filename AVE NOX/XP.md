## Experience points

Experience points can be gained by
1. Hauling treasure from a dungeon delve to a town
2. Defeating monsters
3. Achieaving feats
4. Achieaving a shared session goal, decided at the beginning of each session
5. Carousing in town after a dungeon delve

Expereience points is shared between all members of the party, including retainers.

The division of treasure is agreed by the party and does not influence the amount of XP each character gains. 

See [XP - Carousing](/avenox/AVE%20NOX/XP%20-%20Carousing.html) and [XP - Feats of exploration](/avenox/AVE%20NOX/XP%20-%20Feats%20of%20exploration.html)
